Straight Answers

Which Resource Do I Actually Need?

Is there a free LinkedIn profile analyzer for financial advisors?

Yes. Trained Advisor's LinkedIn Profile Analyzer is free and built specifically for life insurance and annuity advisors. You paste your profile URL and describe your ideal client, and it returns a score out of 100, section-by-section grades for your headline, about, experience, skills, activity and cover photo, ready-to-use rewrites, and a priority action plan. It takes under two minutes and does not require a credit card.

What should I use first if I am starting from scratch?

Run the Profile Analyzer, then read The Polished LinkedIn Growth Playbook. The analyzer tells you what is broken right now; the playbook shows you the order to fix things in. Both take under an hour combined.

Are these resources really free, and what is the catch?

They are free. The guides and the book are delivered by email, so you give us an email address. The training library needs no opt-in at all. Nothing here requires a credit card, and nothing here is a trial that starts billing.

Who are these written for?

Life insurance and annuity advisors, and financial advisors who want a repeatable way to find clients instead of waiting on referrals. The examples, the scripts and the objections are all drawn from that market rather than adapted from generic B2B marketing advice.

I already post on LinkedIn and nothing happens. Which one helps?

Usually the problem is not the posting. Start with the Profile Analyzer, because traffic to a profile that does not name who you help converts badly no matter how good the post was. If your profile scores well, the issue is more likely the offer, so read Making Offers on LinkedIn and run The LinkedIn Offer Scorecard.
